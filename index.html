2Problem We Solve

Independent restaurants still take reservations by phone and paper books. We interviewed owners, hosts and diners and found six recurring pain points:

  • Phone overload — the line is busy during rushes and bookings are lost.
  • Manual allocation errors — tables are promised twice; availability is guessed from memory.
  • No-shows — without reminders, guaranteed covers walk away and tables sit empty.
  • Late cancellations — no waitlist exists to refill the freed table.
  • No operating data — managers cannot see occupancy, peak slots or repeat customers.
  • Platform lock-in — aggregators charge per-cover fees and own the customer relationship.

3Proposed Solution

DineReserve is a self-hosted web system in which diners check real-time table availability, book in under a minute, join a waitlist and receive automatic reminders, while restaurant staff manage the floor plan, check-ins, no-shows and occupancy reports. The system extends the open-source platform TastyIgniter with new automatic data-processing features: waitlist promotion, scheduled reminders, occupancy reporting and a simple no-show risk score.
